Why this shape

  1. 01 · The answer

    The first sentence says what is happening and whether you are needed. No number tiles come before it.

  2. 02 · The handoff

    “Next from Captain” names the next move only when there is one. The page is for looking; direction goes to Captain.

  3. 03 · Current work

    Each agent’s current task appears once. Team can focus on people and availability.

  4. 04 · Goals and proof

    Goals show completed tasks and the next meaningful step. Delivery has one direct path to its record.

  5. 05 · Left out

    No zero counts, models, machine details, review queue, or repeated board. “See all work” opens the deeper view.

Across states

Amber appears only beside a real request to the person. Coral names a problem Captain owns. Empty blocks disappear rather than showing zero.
