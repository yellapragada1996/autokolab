Why it looks like this

Plain-word notes on each choice. Turn on “Eye path” to see the order the eye takes in the first five seconds.

  1. The first five seconds

    0–1 s: the amber dot and the question, if one is waiting. It is the only colour above the heading and the only thing that can block work.

    1–2 s: the heading, the largest text, answers “is anything happening, and on what?”.

    2–4 s: down the column of state words: Working, Reviewing, Idle. They sit at one x position, so the eye runs straight down them.

    4–5 s: the goal counts, quietest of all, for “how far along?”.

  2. Needs you is first, but not loud

    It wins by position and by being the only amber on the page, not by a box or a banner. Each request is a 7 px dot, the question in 17 px, who is waiting and for how long, and where to answer. No filled box, no amber text, no icon (DEC-24).

    At most two requests show, oldest first, so the heading stays on a phone's first screen. The rest fold into “and 1 more in Captain →”. Long questions stop after two lines with “…”.

  3. The heading is the page's answer

    26 px (21 px on phone), weight 500, neutral colour. It is the largest text, so it doesn't need colour or an icon. When an agent stops while it has work, one clause is added: “Tern has stopped.”, so a failure is never only a word in a list.

    There's no visible “Overview” title. The selected tab already says where you are, and a 30 px title would compete with the heading. The title stays for screen readers.

  4. Agents: read down, not across

    Four fixed columns: avatar, name, what it is doing, state. Names get 148 px and cut with “…”; the full name is in the tooltip and read out in full. What it's doing is one line on desktop and up to two on a phone.

    Fixed order: Captain, then by name. A row never moves; only its words change. Idle and offline rows turn grey and lose the dot, so working rows stand out without adding colour.

  5. Being built is the quietest part

    Goal name and “4 of 7 done”, nothing else. Numbers use tabular figures so counts line up. No bar or percentage: a bar suggests a precision that a count of tickets doesn't have. Up to five goals, then “and 1 more in Work →”.

  6. Colour only means status

    Blue working or reviewing · amber needs you · coral stopped or failed · no dot idle or offline. Every dot has a word beside it, and dots are hidden from screen readers because the word already says it. Avatars are neutral grey letters.

  7. Type: one family, four sizes

    Geist. 26 heading, 17 question, 15 rows, 12–13 labels and details. Three weights: 400 text, 500 emphasis, 600 names and labels. Section labels are small capitals in the dimmest grey: they help you find a part but should never be read first.

  8. Space: one 4/8 scale

    64 above the content, 48 between the heading and the first part, 40 between parts, 12 from a label to its list, 4 between rows. Rows are 52 px (64 px on phone). No dividers between rows: the hover fill and even spacing do that job with less ink. One hairline separates Needs you from the rest.

  9. Empty, long and changing

    Empty: a part with nothing in it is not drawn and leaves no gap. Long: names cut on one line, text cuts after two, counts never wrap. All agents always show; idle ones are grey so long lists still scan. Changing: words change in place. A new request pushes the page down because it is the news; scroll anchoring keeps a reader where they are, and a hidden polite message says “1 question needs you”.

  10. Keyboard and screen readers

    Each row is one link and one Tab stop, in reading order, with a 2 px focus ring that passes 3:1. Each part is a section with a heading. A row reads as “Birch, working, adding saved cards”. Hover, pressed and focus look different from each other.

  11. Phone

    Same order, 20 px side margins. An agent row becomes two lines: name and state on top, what it's doing under it. The state word never disappears. Every target is at least 44 px. The bottom bar holds all eight tabs; it scrolls sideways, and a fade at the edge says there's more.

  12. Motion

    Nothing pulses or slides. Only a 120 ms fade on hover and pressed, and it's switched off for people who ask for reduced motion. Loading shows still grey shapes where rows will be, not a spinner.

  14. Left out on purpose

    No page title, no “See all” links (every row opens its detail, and Work and Team are one tab away), no dividers, no counts in labels, no freshness line, no next step from Captain, no delivered list, no tiles. Each would repeat something already on the page.
